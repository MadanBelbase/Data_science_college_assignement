# Difference Between KDD and Data Mining

**KDD (Knowledge Discovery in Databases)** and **Data Mining** are closely related concepts in data science, but they are not the same.

- **KDD:** The overall process of discovering useful, valid, and understandable knowledge from large datasets.
- **Data Mining:** A specific step within the KDD process that uses algorithms and techniques to identify patterns and relationships in data.

## 1. Key Differences

| Basis | KDD | Data Mining |
|---|---|---|
| Full Form | Knowledge Discovery in Databases | Data Mining |
| Meaning | Overall process of discovering knowledge from data. | Process of extracting patterns from data. |
| Scope | Broad process. | A part of KDD. |
| Objective | Discover useful and meaningful knowledge. | Identify hidden patterns and relationships. |
| Process | Includes multiple stages. | Mainly focuses on applying algorithms to data. |
| Techniques | Data cleaning, selection, transformation, mining, and evaluation. | Classification, clustering, regression, association rules, etc. |
| Output | Useful and interpretable knowledge. | Patterns, rules, and models. |
